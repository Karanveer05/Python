NLP Practice Notes

1. Data Cleaning

-   Convert text to lowercase.
-   Remove special characters.
-   Remove numbers (optional).
-   Remove extra spaces.

2. Tokenization

-   Split text into individual words (tokens).

3. Stop Word Removal

-   Remove common words such as: is, the, and, of, in, to.

4. Next NLP Steps

-   Stemming
-   Lemmatization
-   Vectorization (Bag of Words, TF-IDF)

Example

Original: Computer Science Student with knowledge of Python and Java.

After Cleaning: computer science student with knowledge of python and
java

After Tokenization: [‘computer’, ‘science’, ‘student’, ‘with’,
‘knowledge’, ‘of’, ‘python’, ‘and’, ‘java’]

After Stop Word Removal: [‘computer’, ‘science’, ‘student’, ‘knowledge’,
‘python’, ‘java’]
